# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

- First version.
- Requires macOS 26 or later.
- Sound engine: a synthesized friction drum (the Czech fanfrnoch and its relatives, from the
  Brummtopf to the cuíca) in keyboard and pad mode, twelve programs and a default kit; a stroke on
  a drum that sounds runs on from where the head is.
- The app plays Fanfrnoch: from MIDI or the on-screen keyboard and pads, through the audio output you choose.
- Drum One plays every key on one drum, as with one fanfrnoch in your hands; Drum Many gives every note a drum of its own.
- Sixteen pads (notes 36–51), each with a sound and a pitch of its own; the pads of a group are one drum, played by one pair of hands.
- Send to pad copies the keyboard's sound, at the last note played, to a pad.
- Presets (one sound) and kits (all sixteen pads), shared by the app and the plug-in.
- LFO 1, LFO 2 and VEL amounts are set on the knobs themselves; a MIDI controller on every knob and switch, which can learn another with a click.
- Audio Unit instrument with the app's window, installed from the app's Settings.
- The "Modrotisk" look: indigo blueprint cloth, cream floral ornament, a terracotta jug and Liquid Glass cards.
- The app icon: the terracotta fanfrnoch on indigo modrotisk cloth, filling the whole icon shape.
