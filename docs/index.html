Fanfrnoch

A playable synthesized jaw harp: twang, moving overtones, breath and rhythm, as an Audio Unit and a standalone app.

Download for macOS Version 1.0.0 for macOS 26 or later. Read the manual.

What it does

A jaw harp to play

The twang of the reed and the overtones that move as the mouth changes shape, with breath behind them. Seven built-in programs, from the Alpine jaw harp to the Indonesian genggong.

Keys and pads

In keyboard mode every note plays the sound at its pitch. In pad mode sixteen pads each hold a sound and a pitch of their own, with mute groups as on an MPC.

App and Audio Unit

A Mac app with an on-screen keyboard and pads, and an Audio Unit instrument with the same window. Play either from a MIDI keyboard or controller.

Presets, kits and MIDI learn

Save a sound as a preset and all sixteen pads as a kit; the app and the plug-in share them. Every knob follows a MIDI controller and learns a new one with a click.

Download

The disk image is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings. Drag Fanfrnoch to Applications.
