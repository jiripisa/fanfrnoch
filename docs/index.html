Fanfrnoch

A playable synthesized friction drum, after the Czech fanfrnoch: wet-hand strokes on a horsehair drum and its relatives from the Brummtopf to the cuíca, as an Audio Unit and a standalone app.

Download for macOS Version 1.0.0 for macOS 26 or later. Read the manual.

What it does

A friction drum to play

A clay jug covered with a skin, two tufts of horsehair through it, pulled by wet hands: the drum Czech carollers play at Christmas. Twelve built-in programs play it and its relatives: the German Brummtopf, the Spanish zambomba, the Neapolitan putipù, the Brazilian cuíca, the Venezuelan furruco and the Romanian buhai, plus a creak and a flick.

Synthesized, not sampled

Every stroke is made as it sounds, not played back from a recording, so it can move: scoop up into its pitch like the cuíca's whoop, drift as the hand pulls, or jump to a higher mode of the skin. Vary keeps no two strokes quite alike.

Two hands, one drum

A player pulls with both hands in turn, and the weaker one is a little quieter and a little off. A stroke on a sounding drum goes on from where the skin is, so strokes run into each other with a dip, not a click. With Drum One the whole keyboard is one drum; with Drum Many every note is a drum of its own.

Keys and pads

In keyboard mode every note strikes the sound at its pitch. In pad mode notes 36–51 hit sixteen pads, each with a sound, a pitch and a group of its own; the pads of a group are one drum, played by one pair of hands. Send to pad turns a sound made on the keyboard into a pad.

Presets, kits and MIDI learn

Save a sound as a preset and all sixteen pads as a kit; the app and the plug-in share them. Two LFOs and the velocity move the knobs by amounts set on the knobs themselves. Every knob and switch follows a MIDI controller and learns a new one with a click.

App and Audio Unit

A Mac app with an on-screen keyboard and pads, and an Audio Unit instrument with the same window for Logic Pro, Ableton Live and other hosts, installed from the app. The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it only with your OK.

Download

Fanfrnoch needs macOS 26 or later. The disk image is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings. Drag Fanfrnoch to Applications.

Build from source

You need Xcode, for the tool that compiles the app icon; no Xcode project is involved.

git clone https://github.com/jiripisa/fanfrnoch.git
cd fanfrnoch
./run.sh
