# Phase 03 — Panel design: concept 1 "Modrotisk"

**Goal:** the raster artwork the panel of phase 04 is built on, made by Codex, as spec §10 says.

- Codex drew two concepts from `.planning/design/concepts/brief.md`: 1 "Modrotisk" (indigo
  blueprint cloth, cream floral ornament, terracotta jug and arcs) and 2 "Koleda" (smoky wood,
  ochre, red-and-white Chod embroidery). Mockups and Codex's notes in `.planning/design/concepts/`.
- **Chosen: concept 1 [call, delegated by the author].** Reasons: the stronger Czech identity
  (modrotisk), a modern glass look where Koleda reads as a generic wooden plug-in, high contrast
  of cream labels on indigo glass, and warm terracotta arcs that set it apart from Brumle's cyan
  and silver.
